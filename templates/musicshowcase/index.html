About Me
MUSICIAN PROFILE

I am a versatile multi-instrumentalist with formal training and extensive performance experience across multiple genres.

PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY

My musical career began in 2003 when I joined a local death metal band as a drummer. Even in this early role, I demonstrated my multi-instrumental talents by contributing to songwriting and playing guitar. This experience shaped my distinctive approach as a drummer who thinks like a composer. In 2009, I reached a significant milestone when I joined the established power metal band Axenstar as their drummer, recording for their album and gaining valuable studio experience. My professional development was significantly influenced by two important mentors. Local drummer Svetlan Råket (known for his work with Zello, Par Lindh Project, Lisa LaRue Project 2K9, and Timecode Alpha) inspired my decision to further my education in Los Angeles. Additionally, world-renowned Los Angeles-based drummer Joey Heredia provided invaluable private instruction and mentorship, substantially enhancing my technical proficiency and musical perspective.

EDUCATION & TRAINING

After showing early promise as a musician, I pursued professional training at the Los Angeles College of Music, where I earned my Associate of Arts in Drums (2015-2017). During this intensive program, I had the privilege of studying under world-renowned drummers including Ralph Humphrey, Joe Porcaro, Joey Heredia, and Gary Ferguson. My curriculum encompassed both technical and theoretical aspects of music. I developed advanced skills in sight reading, transcription, specialized playing techniques, and complex rhythm studies. Beyond percussion, I expanded my musical foundation through courses in music theory, piano proficiency, recording technology, and music business practices.

GENRE EXPERTISE

My formal education provided me with versatility across numerous musical styles including Roots/Blues, Rock/Pop, Jazz, Big Band, World Music, Contemporary, and complex compositions featuring odd meters and advanced rhythmic structures.

CURRENT PROJECTS

After returning to Sweden in 2018, I methodically built my personal recording studio, creating a professional space for composition and production. This environment supports my current collaboration with Mattias Tiselius (of The Drake Equation) in our band Agent Bonfire, which serves as our creative outlet for musical exploration and expression. In recent years, I've expanded my skill set to include music production and mixing, mastering the technical aspects of bringing compositions from concept to finished product.

MUSICAL PHILOSOPHY

At my core, I identify as a songwriter, approaching percussion with a composer's sensibility rather than merely as a timekeeper. Throughout my career, I've cultivated my abilities on piano, deepening my understanding of harmony and expanding my compositional capabilities. This integrative approach allows me to write authentically across diverse musical styles, bringing a unique perspective to each project I undertake.
